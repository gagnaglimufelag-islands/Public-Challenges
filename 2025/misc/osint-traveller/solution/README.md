# Solution

The hint in the handout limits the locations we needed to search for European major cities. Looking at the provided picture, this country has many scooters and the driver is driving on the left-hand side of the car. If we look up all road signage in Europe, we can use the blue sign with an adult and a child on it (Pedestrian zone) to narrow down our search to three countries. Sweden, Luxemburg or France. We can then immediately eliminate Sweden as the photo is quite clearly not from there. 

Further utilizing the road signage we can see that the no parking sign on the photo is much closer to the French sign than the Luxemburg one due to the different blue hues. This means that we have likely found our country.

In the background of the photo, we can see a large building that looks like a church or cathedral. We therefore need to search for a building with one large dome, two smaller ones right next to it likely in the same building and a tower that is nearby. A search of for an example "France catholic shrines" reveals multiple searches with this church in it.

Utilizing street view or pictures of the church, we must be looking at the west ish side of the building. That means that the photo must be taken somewhere east of it. Then it should be easier to narrow down which train station is nearby to the building.
